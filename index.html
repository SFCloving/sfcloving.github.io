Can I join if…?

The questions people are afraid to ask.

Every situation is different, so these are honest starting points, not final answers. Final eligibility is decided during processing. Ask me about yours.

…I have tattoos?

Usually, yes. Where they are and what they show matters. Snap a few photos and I'll tell you where you stand.

…I have a GED, not a diploma?

People with a GED do join. Some options depend on your test scores and credits, so let's look at yours together.

…I have asthma or another medical issue?

Some conditions are fine, some can be waived, and some are disqualifying. A doctor makes the call during processing. I'll be straight with you before you go.

…I'm not at my goal weight?

There are height, weight, and body-fat standards. If you're close, there are prep programs to help you get there. Many people start right where you are.

…I'm planning on college?

Good. The Army can help pay for college before, during, or after service. I'll show you what that could look like for you.

…I'm 17, or I'm older?

Age limits apply, and 17-year-olds need a parent's consent. If you're older and wondering whether you've missed your window, ask. You might not have.

…I've been in trouble before?

It depends on what and when. Be upfront with me and I'll tell you honestly what's possible.

…I just want information?

That's what the 15 minutes is for. No commitment and no paperwork. You leave with answers.

For parents

You should be in the room.

Most families I sit with have the same questions. I'd rather answer them with you there than have your son or daughter try to explain it later.

  • What would they actually do? We look at specific jobs, not just "the Army."
  • How does college get paid for? I'll walk you through the options.
  • What are the commitments? Length of service, training, and what happens after.
  • Is it safe to just talk? Yes. A conversation doesn't commit anyone to anything.
